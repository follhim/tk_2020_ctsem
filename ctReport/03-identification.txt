Read: a condition number above about 1e6, a non-positive eigenvalue, or
any weak direction means the data do not pin every parameter. Estimates
can then be moved along that direction without worsening the fit, so
their intervals describe a shape the data did not choose. The ratio
above says the same thing per parameter: how much wider the reported
interval is than that parameter's own curvature supports. Healthy fits
sit near 1; a ratio of 100 means the width is entanglement with the
other parameters, and it will not repeat between runs.
